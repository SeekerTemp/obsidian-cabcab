# Shadow Mage

The Shadow Mage is a high-risk, high-reward spellcaster of the Arcane Circle.

## Background

Exiled from the main academy for forbidden research into void energies, the Shadow Mage now serves as a mercenary.

## Abilities

- **Shadow Bolt** — Ranged magical attack with 30% chance to silence
- **Void Step** — Teleport to any dark tile within range
- **Drain Life** — Steals HP from a target and converts it to mana
