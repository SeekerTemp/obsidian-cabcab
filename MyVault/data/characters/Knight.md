# Knight

The Knight is one of the core frontline fighters of the Order of the Iron Shield.

## Background

Trained from youth in the art of combat and discipline, the Knight embodies strength and honor.

## Abilities

- **Shield Bash** — Stuns an enemy for 1 turn
- **Charge** — Deals bonus damage when moving before attacking
- **Iron Will** — Reduces incoming damage by 10% when HP is below 50%
