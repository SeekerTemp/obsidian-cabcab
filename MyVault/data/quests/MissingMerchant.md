# The Missing Merchant

A local elder asks you to investigate the disappearance of a merchant on the eastern road.

## Objectives

- [ ] Speak with Elder Voss at the town hall
- [ ] Investigate the last known location on the eastern road
- [ ] Find clues about the merchant's fate
- [ ] Report back to Elder Voss

## Notes

The merchant was carrying a shipment of alchemical supplies. Bandits have been reported in the area recently.
